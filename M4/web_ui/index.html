02

Algorithmic Baseline Formulation

Instead of naive mean & standard deviation (which are easily corrupted by outlier spikes), the baseline uses:

  • Rolling 28-Day Median (μ̃₂₈): Resistant to up to 50% contamination in the lookback window.
  • Median Absolute Deviation (MAD): σ_MAD = 1.4826 × median(|xᵢ - μ̃|) for normal distribution consistency.
  • Statistical Noise Floor: σ_eff = max(σ_MAD, √μ̃_Poisson, Floor_min) to protect low-volume counts.
  • 14-Day Freeze Memory: Detected true anomalies are excluded from future baselines to prevent gradual baseline decay.
03

Severity Scoring & Routing Engine

Alert routing is determined dynamically using multi-factor severity scoring:

SeverityScore = TypeWeight × min(|DeviationPct| / 100, 3.0) × SpendShareWeight
  • Immediate Pager Stream: Dispatched immediately for high severity (Score ≥ 0.60) or core outage incidents (Tracking Break, Budget Outage).
  • Daily Digest Stream: Batched into morning summary reports for lower-severity drift (Score < 0.60) and peer-seasonal movements.
04

Edge Case & Boundary Stress Testing

  • Low Conversion Volume Campaigns: Poisson floor (√λ) prevents small accounts (e.g. 2 conv/day) from falsely alerting when 0 occurs.
  • Account-Wide Seasonal Days (e.g., Black Friday): Peer correlation filter evaluates cohort deviation; if ≥ 40% of campaigns move together, routing switches from Pager to Digest.
  • Zero Denominator Protection: CVR and CPA metrics enforce a minimum click/conversion threshold before computing division.
